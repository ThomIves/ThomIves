6. The 6 most likely questions — answer shapes

  1. "Tell me about yourself" → the 60-sec opening above
  2. "Why NEOGOV / why now" → the 30-sec answer above
  3. "Walk me through a production AI system end-to-end" → DEFAULT TO THE 10-STEP TEMPLATE. Do NOT narrow to a piece. This is the exact failure that cost you at PG&E — do not repeat. Use Echo as the walkthrough (revenue-relevant to her).
  4. "First 90 days if you joined?" → "Not by proposing architecture. Time with your sellers, time with your RevOps team, understanding the public-safety cross-sell picture. Context first, architecture second."
  5. "Assistive vs. agentic AI?" → Agree with her (assistive is right for revenue systems). "Reps lose customer trust once, that customer is gone. Assistive amplifies seller judgment, agentic substitutes. Assistive done right requires the same rigor — evaluation loops, drift monitoring, versioned releases."
  6. "How do you measure whether the AI is working?" → Two layers. Engineering (precision/recall/drift/cost/latency) matters for engineering; revenue velocity change on AI-touched pipeline, forecast accuracy delta, seller-hours saved, cross-sell attach rate in the public-safety stack matters for her seat. Instrument both from day one.
